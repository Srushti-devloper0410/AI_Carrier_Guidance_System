What is Engineering?

Engineering applies mathematics, science and technology to design, develop, test and improve products, structures, machines, systems and processes.

Understanding the Engineering Industry

Engineers work in construction, manufacturing, transportation, energy, electronics, software, aerospace, automation and many other industries.

They identify problems, develop designs, test solutions and improve systems to make them safer, more efficient and more useful.

Who can choose Engineering?

  • Students interested in mathematics and science.
  • Students who enjoy building and designing things.
  • Students interested in machines, electronics or technology.
  • Students who enjoy logical thinking and problem-solving.
  • Students interested in innovation and practical projects.

Education Pathway in Engineering

Your pathway depends on the engineering branch and admission requirements of the institution.

After 10th

  • Choose Science with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics (PCM) for the 11th–12th engineering route.
  • Consider a Diploma in Engineering and Technology after SSC.
  • Explore branches such as Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Computer and Electronics.
  • Check the current diploma admission eligibility.

After 12th

  • B.E. - Bachelor of Engineering.
  • B.Tech - Bachelor of Technology.
  • Choose a branch such as Computer, Civil, Mechanical or Electrical Engineering.
  • Other specialised engineering degrees offered by recognised institutions.

After Graduation

  • M.E. - Master of Engineering.
  • M.Tech - Master of Technology.
  • Specialisation in Robotics, AI, Structural Engineering or other fields.
  • Research, higher studies and professional certifications.

Entrance Exams and Admissions

1. MHT-CET (PCM)

Maharashtra's state-level entrance examination used for admission to eligible engineering and technology degree courses.

2. JEE Main

National-level entrance examination used for admission to NITs, IIITs and other participating engineering institutions, subject to their rules.

3. JEE Advanced

Entrance examination for admission to IIT undergraduate programmes. Candidates must first satisfy the applicable JEE Main and other eligibility rules.

4. Diploma Admissions

Maharashtra conducts a separate post-SSC diploma admission process through the Directorate of Technical Education (DTE).

5. GATE

Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering is used by many institutions for postgraduate engineering admissions and by some organisations for recruitment.

Important Note

Entrance exams, accepted subjects, marks and eligibility vary by course, institution and admission year. Always check the latest official admission brochure.

Skills Required for Engineering

Technical Skills

  • Mathematics and scientific fundamentals.
  • Engineering drawing and design concepts.
  • Technical software and computer applications.
  • Knowledge of the selected engineering branch.
  • Testing, measurement and analysis.
  • Practical project development.

Personal Skills

  • Logical and analytical thinking.
  • Problem-solving ability.
  • Creativity and innovation.
  • Teamwork and communication.
  • Time management.
  • Attention to safety and detail.

How to Build a Career in Engineering?

  1. Explore different engineering branches.
  2. Choose a suitable educational pathway.
  3. Understand the eligibility and entrance requirements.
  4. Complete a recognised engineering degree or diploma.
  5. Develop practical and technical skills.
  6. Build projects and gain industry experience.
  7. Complete internships or relevant training.
  8. Apply for suitable engineering opportunities or higher studies.
