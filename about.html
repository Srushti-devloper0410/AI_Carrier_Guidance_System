ABOUT THE PROJECT

Making Career Guidance More Personalized

AI Career Guide is an AI-based personalized career guidance system designed to help students explore suitable career paths based on their individual profile.

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Helping Students Make Informed Career Decisions

Choosing a suitable career can be challenging for students because they may have limited knowledge about different career opportunities.

The proposed system uses an AI-based recommendation approach to analyze factors such as interests, skills, academic performance and preferences.

Based on this analysis, the system provides personalized career recommendations and supports students with skill development guidance.

🤖

AI-Based Guidance

Analyze student information and assessment responses to identify suitable career paths.

🎯

Personalized Recommendations

Career suggestions are based on individual interests, skills and academic profile.

🛣️

Skill Development

Students can identify required skills and follow a structured learning roadmap.

OUR GOAL

From Career Confusion to Career Direction

The system aims to make career guidance more accessible, structured and personalized for students. It combines AI, web technology and data analysis to support students in exploring suitable career opportunities.
