About Law & Legal Services

Law is a field concerned with justice, rights, legal rules, dispute resolution and the functioning of the legal system. Legal professionals work with individuals, companies, courts, government organisations and institutions. Career opportunities include advocacy, judiciary, corporate legal services, legal research, compliance and public service.

Educational Pathways

After 10th

Choose Arts, Commerce or Science for 11th and 12th. All streams can lead to law, provided you meet the relevant admission requirements.

Foundation Level

After 12th

Apply for a 5-year integrated law degree such as BA LL.B., BBA LL.B., B.Com LL.B. or another recognised integrated programme.

5-Year Law Degree

After Graduation

Graduates from any recognised discipline can explore a 3-year LL.B. programme. Specialised postgraduate study such as LL.M. is also available after LL.B.

3-Year LL.B.

Entrance Exams & Legal Qualification

CLAT

Common Law Admission Test for admission to participating National Law Universities, primarily for undergraduate and postgraduate law programmes.

National Level

MHT CET Law

Maharashtra entrance examination for admissions to participating colleges offering 5-year and 3-year LL.B. programmes.

Maharashtra

CUET / University Admissions

Some institutions use CUET or their own admission processes for law-related or university programmes. Requirements vary by institution.

University Admissions

State Judicial Exams

Judicial service examinations recruit eligible candidates for judicial positions. Qualifications, experience and rules depend on the latest state notification.

Judiciary

Bar Council Enrollment

Graduates seeking to practise law must meet applicable enrolment and qualification requirements, including the relevant bar examination rules.

Your Law Career Roadmap

Step 1: Explore Legal Careers

Identify your interest in advocacy, corporate law, judiciary, legal research, public service or compliance.

Step 2: Choose Your Law Degree

Select a 5-year integrated LL.B. after 12th or a 3-year LL.B. after graduation.

Step 3: Prepare for Admission

Check entrance exams, eligibility, application dates and admission procedures of your target institutions.

Step 4: Develop Legal Skills

Build legal research, drafting, communication, analytical thinking and knowledge of legal procedures.

Step 5: Gain Practical Experience

Participate in internships, moot courts, legal aid activities, research projects and practical training.

Step 6: Choose Your Specialisation

Focus on your preferred area and pursue the necessary qualifications, enrolment or examinations for your chosen profession.
