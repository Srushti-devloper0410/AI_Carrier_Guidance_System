What is Healthcare?

Healthcare is a field focused on maintaining health, preventing diseases, diagnosing medical conditions, providing treatment and supporting recovery.

Understanding the Healthcare Industry

Healthcare professionals work in hospitals, clinics, laboratories, pharmacies, rehabilitation centres, research institutions and community health services.

The field includes doctors, nurses, pharmacists, physiotherapists, laboratory professionals, nutrition experts and many other specialists.

Who can choose Healthcare?

  • Students interested in human biology and health.
  • Students who want to help and care for people.
  • Students interested in medical science and research.
  • Students who are patient, responsible and attentive.
  • Students interested in diagnosis, treatment or rehabilitation.

Education Pathway in Healthcare

Your education pathway depends on the healthcare profession you want to enter. Different courses have different subject and admission requirements.

After 10th

  • Choose Science with Biology if interested in medical and many clinical pathways.
  • Study Physics, Chemistry and Biology in 11th and 12th for NEET-based medical courses.
  • Explore recognised diploma or vocational healthcare options where eligible.
  • Check course-specific requirements before admission.

After 12th

  • MBBS - Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery.
  • BDS - Bachelor of Dental Surgery.
  • B.Sc. Nursing - Nursing degree.
  • B.Pharm - Bachelor of Pharmacy.
  • BPT - Bachelor of Physiotherapy.
  • B.Sc. Medical Laboratory Technology or related courses.
  • Relevant degrees in Nutrition, Psychology and Allied Health Sciences.

After Graduation

  • MD / MS after eligible medical qualifications.
  • MDS after BDS and required eligibility.
  • M.Sc. Nursing after eligible nursing qualification.
  • M.Pharm after eligible pharmacy qualification.
  • MPT after eligible physiotherapy qualification.
  • Specialised postgraduate courses and research programmes.

Entrance Exams and Admissions

1. NEET-UG

NEET-UG is the national entrance examination for undergraduate medical courses such as MBBS, BDS, BAMS, BHMS, BUMS and BSMS. Candidates must meet the applicable eligibility conditions and admission requirements.

2. MH-Nursing CET

Maharashtra State CET Cell conducts MH-Nursing CET for admission to eligible B.Sc. Nursing and GNM courses as specified in the current admission brochure.

3. MHT-CET (PCB / PCM)

MHT-CET has relevant admission routes for pharmacy courses. The accepted subject group and eligibility depend on the course and current Maharashtra admission rules.

4. Other Healthcare and Allied Health Courses

Admissions to physiotherapy, medical laboratory, radiology, nutrition and other allied-health programmes may depend on the institution, university and applicable state regulations. Some may have entrance tests, while others may use qualifying examination merit.

Important Note

NEET is not required for every healthcare career. Always check the latest official admission brochure, recognised course requirements and professional registration rules before choosing a course.

Skills Required for Healthcare Careers

Knowledge and Professional Skills

  • Basic understanding of human biology.
  • Scientific thinking and observation.
  • Knowledge of healthcare practices relevant to the role.
  • Accuracy and attention to detail.
  • Patient safety and professional responsibility.
  • Continuous learning and practical training.

Personal Skills

  • Communication skills.
  • Empathy and patience.
  • Teamwork and cooperation.
  • Problem-solving ability.
  • Time management.
  • Ethical decision-making.

How to Build a Career in Healthcare?

  1. Explore different healthcare professions.
  2. Identify the course that matches your interests.
  3. Understand the required subjects and eligibility.
  4. Prepare for the relevant entrance examination, if required.
  5. Complete the recognised degree or diploma.
  6. Complete required practical training or internship.
  7. Meet applicable professional registration requirements.
  8. Apply for suitable healthcare opportunities or higher studies.
