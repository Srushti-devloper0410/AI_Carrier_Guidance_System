What is Design & Creative Arts?

Design is the process of creating visual, digital and physical solutions by combining creativity, communication and practical skills.

Understanding the Creative Industry

Creative professionals develop visual identities, digital experiences, products, buildings, animations, clothing and other creative solutions.

They work in design studios, advertising agencies, media companies, fashion houses, technology companies and as independent professionals.

Who can choose this field?

  • Students who enjoy drawing and creative activities.
  • Students interested in colours, layouts and visual communication.
  • Students who enjoy designing digital products.
  • Students interested in fashion, animation or photography.
  • Students who enjoy imagining and developing new ideas.

Education Pathway in Design

Different creative professions have different academic and portfolio requirements.

After 10th

  • Choose Arts, Commerce or Science according to your interests and future course requirements.
  • Explore diploma courses in graphic design, animation, fashion or applied arts.
  • For architecture, consider the Science with Mathematics pathway.
  • Develop drawing, creativity and observation skills.

After 12th

  • B.Des - Bachelor of Design.
  • BFA - Bachelor of Fine Arts.
  • B.Arch - Bachelor of Architecture.
  • B.Sc. Animation and Multimedia.
  • B.Des in Fashion or Product Design.
  • Relevant degrees in Interior and Communication Design.

After Graduation

  • M.Des - Master of Design.
  • MFA - Master of Fine Arts.
  • M.Arch - Master of Architecture.
  • Specialised postgraduate design programmes.
  • Professional design certifications and portfolio development.

Entrance Exams and Admissions

1. UCEED

Undergraduate Common Entrance Examination for Design is used by participating institutions for admission to eligible B.Des programmes.

2. NID DAT

National Institute of Design conducts the Design Aptitude Test for admission to its eligible undergraduate and postgraduate design programmes.

3. NIFT Entrance Examination

NIFT conducts entrance examinations for admission to its design, fashion and related programmes as specified in its admission notification.

4. NATA

National Aptitude Test in Architecture is an entrance examination used for eligible B.Arch admissions. JEE Main Paper 2 is another recognised route at participating institutions.

Important Note

Not every design course requires an entrance examination. Eligibility, accepted subjects and selection processes differ by institution and programme. Check the latest official brochure.

Skills Required for Design Careers

Technical Skills

  • Drawing and visualisation.
  • Colour theory and typography.
  • Design software and digital tools.
  • Layout and composition.
  • Prototyping and design presentation.
  • Photography and editing where relevant.

Personal Skills

  • Creativity and imagination.
  • Observation and attention to detail.
  • Communication and presentation.
  • Problem-solving.
  • Time management.
  • Adaptability and willingness to learn.

How to Build a Career in Design?

  1. Explore different design specialisations.
  2. Identify the area that matches your creative interests.
  3. Choose a suitable course and check eligibility.
  4. Learn relevant design software and techniques.
  5. Create original projects and build a portfolio.
  6. Participate in design competitions and practical projects.
  7. Complete internships or gain project experience.
  8. Apply for suitable design opportunities or higher studies.
