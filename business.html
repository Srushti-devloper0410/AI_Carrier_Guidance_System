What is Business & Management?

Business and Management is a field focused on planning, organising, operating and growing businesses and organisations.

Understanding the Business Industry

Every organisation needs people who can manage resources, understand customers, plan strategies, handle finances, coordinate teams and improve business performance.

Business professionals work in companies, banks, startups, consulting firms, retail organisations, manufacturing industries and government organisations.

Who can choose Business & Management?

  • Students interested in business and entrepreneurship.
  • Students who enjoy communication and teamwork.
  • Students interested in planning and organising activities.
  • Students who enjoy analysing business problems.
  • Students interested in marketing, finance or leadership.
  • Students who want to start or manage their own business.

Education Pathway in Business

Business careers are open to students from different educational backgrounds. The suitable course depends on your chosen specialisation and institution.

After 10th

  • Commerce is a useful stream for learning Accountancy, Economics and Business Studies.
  • Arts and Science students can also explore business-related degrees, subject to admission eligibility.
  • Develop basic communication, mathematics and computer skills.
  • Learn about entrepreneurship and how businesses work.

After 12th

  • BBA - Bachelor of Business Administration.
  • BMS - Bachelor of Management Studies.
  • BBM - Bachelor of Business Management.
  • B.Com - Bachelor of Commerce.
  • BA Economics or related programmes.
  • Integrated management programmes offered by eligible institutions.

After Graduation

  • MBA - Master of Business Administration.
  • MMS - Master of Management Studies.
  • M.Com - Master of Commerce.
  • PGDM - Post Graduate Diploma in Management.
  • Specialisations in Marketing, HR, Finance and Operations.
  • Professional certifications and entrepreneurship programmes.

Entrance Exams and Admissions

1. MAH-BBA/BMS/BBM CET

Maharashtra State CET Cell conducts a common entrance examination for BBA, BMS and BBM, along with certain other undergraduate programmes. Admission eligibility depends on the affiliating university and current admission rules.

2. CUET-UG

The Common University Entrance Test is conducted by NTA for undergraduate admissions at participating universities. Available programmes and subject requirements differ by university.

3. IPMAT

The Integrated Programme in Management Aptitude Test is used by certain institutions for admission to their integrated management programmes. Eligibility and selection rules are institution-specific.

4. MAH-MBA/MMS CET

Maharashtra conducts this entrance examination for eligible postgraduate MBA/MMS admissions. Candidates must meet the applicable graduation and admission requirements.

5. CAT

The Common Admission Test is used by IIMs and other participating management institutions for postgraduate management admissions. Each institution has its own selection criteria.

Important Note

Not every business course requires an entrance exam. Some institutions offer admission based on qualifying examination marks, while others conduct entrance tests. Always check the latest official admission brochure.

Skills Required for Business Careers

Professional Skills

  • Business and market understanding.
  • Basic financial and numerical knowledge.
  • Planning and organisational skills.
  • Marketing and customer relationship skills.
  • Data interpretation and analytical thinking.
  • Computer and business software knowledge.

Personal Skills

  • Communication and presentation.
  • Leadership and teamwork.
  • Decision-making.
  • Problem-solving.
  • Negotiation and interpersonal skills.
  • Time management and adaptability.
  • Creativity and initiative.

How to Build a Career in Business?

  1. Explore business and management career options.
  2. Identify your interests, such as marketing, HR, finance or entrepreneurship.
  3. Choose a suitable stream and undergraduate course.
  4. Understand the admission and entrance exam requirements.
  5. Develop communication, analytical and management skills.
  6. Take part in projects, business activities and internships.
  7. Gain practical experience through suitable opportunities.
  8. Choose higher studies or apply for relevant entry-level roles.
