About Finance & Banking

Finance and banking focus on managing money, financial records, investments, banking services, business funds and financial decisions. This field offers career opportunities in banks, companies, financial institutions, insurance organisations, investment firms and government departments.

Educational Pathways

After 10th

Choose Commerce if you are interested in accounts, economics, business, banking and financial subjects. Other streams can also lead to many finance careers.

Foundation Level

After 12th

Explore B.Com, BBA, BMS, Economics and other relevant undergraduate courses. Professional courses such as CA and CMA have their own entry pathways.

Undergraduate Studies

After Graduation

Consider MBA Finance, M.Com, professional certifications or banking recruitment exams depending on your career goal.

Higher Education

Important Exams & Certifications

CA – Chartered Accountant

Professional qualification in accounting, auditing, taxation and financial management through the ICAI course pathway.

Professional Course

CMA – Cost Management Accounting

Professional course focusing on cost accounting, management accounting, finance and business decision-making.

Professional Course

CS – Company Secretary

Professional qualification focused on corporate law, governance, compliance and company administration.

Corporate Compliance

IBPS Exams

Recruitment examinations for eligible banking positions in participating public sector banks.

Banking Recruitment

SBI Recruitment Exams

Recruitment processes for SBI positions such as PO and Junior Associate, according to eligibility.

Banking Recruitment

NISM Certifications

Industry certifications covering areas such as securities markets, investment products and financial services.

Finance Certification
