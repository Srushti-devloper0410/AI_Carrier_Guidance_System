What is Agriculture?

Agriculture involves cultivating crops, managing livestock, producing food and developing sustainable farming systems.

Understanding the Agriculture Industry

Agriculture supports food security, rural development, food processing, trade and the economy. Modern agriculture also uses technology, scientific research and data to improve productivity.

Professionals work on farms, research centres, agricultural companies, food industries, government departments and rural development organisations.

Who can choose Agriculture?

  • Students interested in plants, crops and nature.
  • Students interested in biology and environmental science.
  • Students who enjoy practical and field-based work.
  • Students interested in food production and sustainability.
  • Students interested in agricultural technology and research.

Education Pathway in Agriculture

Agriculture has different pathways in crop science, engineering, animal science and food technology.

After 10th

  • Choose Science with Biology for many agricultural science pathways.
  • Explore Agriculture as a subject in eligible higher-secondary programmes.
  • Consider recognised agricultural or vocational diploma courses.
  • Develop knowledge of plants, soil and the environment.

After 12th

  • B.Sc. Agriculture.
  • B.Sc. Horticulture.
  • B.Sc. Forestry.
  • B.Tech Agricultural Engineering.
  • B.V.Sc. & A.H. Veterinary Science.
  • B.Tech Food Technology and related programmes.

After Graduation

  • M.Sc. Agriculture and specialisations.
  • M.Sc. Horticulture.
  • M.Tech Agricultural Engineering.
  • MVSc - Master of Veterinary Science.
  • Research and doctoral programmes.
  • Specialised courses in agribusiness and food science.

Entrance Exams and Admissions

1. MHT-CET (PCB / PCM)

Relevant Maharashtra admission routes may use MHT-CET scores for specified agricultural and allied undergraduate programmes, depending on the current admission rules.

2. CUET-UG

Some participating universities offer agriculture-related undergraduate programmes through CUET-UG. Course availability and subject requirements vary.

3. ICAR AIEEA (PG)

ICAR's national-level entrance examination is used for eligible postgraduate agriculture and allied science admissions at participating institutions.

4. Veterinary Admissions

Veterinary degree admissions follow applicable NEET-UG and counselling rules. Check the current official veterinary admission notification.

Important Note

Entrance examinations and eligibility differ by course and institution. Do not assume every agriculture course requires the same exam. Verify the current Maharashtra and university admission brochures.

Skills Required for Agriculture Careers

Technical Skills

  • Knowledge of crops, soil and plant science.
  • Agricultural machinery and technology.
  • Research and field observation.
  • Data collection and analysis.
  • Environmental and resource management.
  • Food safety and quality concepts.

Personal Skills

  • Problem-solving.
  • Observation and patience.
  • Communication and teamwork.
  • Practical thinking.
  • Adaptability.
  • Interest in sustainable development.

How to Build a Career in Agriculture?

  1. Explore agriculture and allied science career options.
  2. Choose a suitable subject combination and course.
  3. Understand the admission requirements.
  4. Develop scientific and practical agricultural knowledge.
  5. Participate in fieldwork, laboratory work and projects.
  6. Complete required practical training.
  7. Gain experience through internships or research.
  8. Apply for relevant jobs or higher studies.
