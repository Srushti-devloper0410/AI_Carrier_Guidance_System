What is Information Technology?

Information Technology (IT) is a field that uses computers, software, networks and digital systems to store, process, manage and share information.

Understanding the IT Industry

The IT industry plays an important role in almost every sector, including banking, healthcare, education, business, communication and entertainment.

IT professionals develop websites and applications, manage databases, protect digital information, analyse data and maintain computer networks.

Who can choose IT?

  • Students interested in computers and technology.
  • Students who enjoy logical thinking and problem-solving.
  • Students interested in designing websites and applications.
  • Students who enjoy learning new digital tools.
  • Students interested in data, security or networking.

Education Pathway in IT

There are different ways to enter the IT field. Choose an educational pathway according to your interests and the eligibility criteria of the course.

After 10th

  • Choose Science (PCM) in 11th and 12th for an engineering pathway.
  • Consider a Diploma in Computer Engineering or Information Technology.
  • Other streams may also offer computing-related degree options, depending on admission rules.

After 12th

  • BCA - Bachelor of Computer Applications.
  • B.Sc. Computer Science or Information Technology.
  • B.E. / B.Tech in Computer Science or IT.
  • Other relevant computing and technology degrees.

After Graduation

  • MCA - Master of Computer Applications.
  • M.Sc. Computer Science or IT.
  • M.Tech in a relevant specialisation.
  • Professional certifications and practical training.

Entrance Exams and Admissions

1. MHT-CET (PCM)

Used for admission to eligible engineering and technology courses in Maharashtra. Physics and Mathematics requirements apply as per the current admission rules.

2. JEE Main

An important national-level entrance examination for admission to participating engineering institutions, subject to their eligibility and admission rules.

3. MAH-BCA/BBA/BMS/BBM CET

Maharashtra CET Cell conducts the common entrance test for BCA and certain related undergraduate courses. Check the current brochure and university eligibility.

4. Diploma Admission

Maharashtra offers admission to eligible students for post-SSC diploma courses through the official DTE admission process.

Important: Not every IT career requires an entrance examination. Admission requirements differ according to the course, institution, category and academic year. Always check the latest official brochure.

How to Build a Career in IT?

  1. Explore different IT career options.
  2. Choose a suitable education pathway.
  3. Learn the technical skills required for your chosen role.
  4. Build practical projects and a portfolio.
  5. Practise problem-solving and communication.
  6. Complete relevant internships or practical training.
  7. Apply for suitable entry-level opportunities.
