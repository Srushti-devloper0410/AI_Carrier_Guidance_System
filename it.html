What is Information Technology?

Information Technology is a field that involves computers, software, networks and digital systems to store, process, manage and share information.

Understanding the IT Industry

The IT industry plays a major role in almost every sector, including banking, healthcare, education, business, communication and entertainment.

IT professionals create websites and applications, manage databases, analyse information, maintain networks and protect digital systems.

Who can choose IT?

  • Students interested in computers and technology.
  • Students who enjoy logical thinking and problem-solving.
  • Students interested in creating websites and applications.
  • Students who enjoy learning digital tools.
  • Students interested in software, data or cybersecurity.

Entrance Exams & Admission Options

Admission to IT-related courses depends on the course, institution and eligibility requirements.

1. MHT-CET (PCM)

Maharashtra entrance examination used for admission to eligible engineering courses such as Computer Engineering and Information Technology.

2. JEE Main

A national-level entrance examination for admission to participating engineering colleges and institutions according to their admission criteria.

3. MAH-BCA/BBA/BMS/BBM CET

Maharashtra Common Entrance Test for admission to BCA and certain related undergraduate courses in participating institutions.

4. University-Level Admission

Many colleges and universities offer BCA, B.Sc. Computer Science and IT courses through merit-based admission, entrance examinations or their own admission process.

5. Diploma Admission

Students can pursue diploma courses in Computer Engineering or Information Technology after 10th through the applicable Maharashtra diploma admission process.

Important: Entrance examinations are not compulsory for every IT course. Admission criteria, eligibility and entrance requirements differ by institution and academic year.

How to Build a Career in IT?

Follow these steps to start preparing for your future career in the Information Technology industry.

  1. Explore different IT career roles and identify your interests.
  2. Choose an appropriate educational pathway.
  3. Learn programming fundamentals and required technical skills.
  4. Build projects and create a practical portfolio.
  5. Improve communication and problem-solving abilities.
  6. Gain experience through internships and practical training.
  7. Prepare your resume and apply for entry-level opportunities.
