What Our System Provides

Students can provide their academic details, skills, interests and career preferences to create a personalized profile.

📝

Career Assessment

An assessment questionnaire collects information about students' interests, skills and preferred career areas.

🤖

AI-Based Analysis

The recommendation algorithm analyzes student responses and identifies suitable career paths.

🎯

Career Recommendations

Students receive personalized career suggestions based on their profile and assessment responses.

The system identifies important skills that students may need to develop for their selected career path.

Learning Roadmap

Students receive a structured learning roadmap containing skills and learning areas relevant to their career goal.
