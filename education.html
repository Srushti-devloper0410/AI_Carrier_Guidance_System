What is the Education Field?

Education focuses on teaching, learning, developing knowledge and helping individuals build academic and practical skills.

Understanding the Education Sector

Education professionals work in schools, colleges, universities, coaching institutions, training centres, research organisations and digital learning platforms.

The sector includes teachers, professors, counsellors, special educators, researchers, curriculum developers and education technology professionals.

Who can choose Education?

  • Students who enjoy explaining concepts.
  • Students interested in learning and research.
  • Students who are patient and good communicators.
  • Students interested in child development.
  • Students who want to contribute to society through education.

Education Pathway in Teaching and Education

The qualifications required depend on whether you want to teach at school, college or university level.

After 10th

  • Choose Arts, Commerce or Science based on the subject you want to teach later.
  • Develop communication and language skills.
  • Explore recognised vocational and educational support courses.
  • Build a strong foundation in your favourite subjects.

After 12th

  • Integrated B.A. B.Ed. or B.Sc. B.Ed. where offered.
  • Relevant undergraduate degree in your chosen subject.
  • Integrated teacher education programmes.
  • Early childhood education programmes with recognised eligibility.

After Graduation

  • B.Ed. after an eligible bachelor's degree.
  • M.Ed. - Master of Education.
  • Master's degree in your teaching subject.
  • Ph.D. for academic research and eligible university positions.
  • Special education qualifications for relevant roles.

Entrance Exams and Eligibility

1. MAH-B.Ed CET

Maharashtra conducts this entrance examination for admission to eligible B.Ed programmes. Candidates must meet the current graduation and admission conditions.

2. MAH-M.Ed CET

This entrance examination is used for eligible M.Ed admissions in Maharashtra as specified by the CET Cell.

3. CTET

The Central Teacher Eligibility Test assesses eligibility for specified teaching levels in schools under applicable central government rules.

4. MAHA TET

Maharashtra Teacher Eligibility Test is relevant to eligibility for specified school teaching appointments in the state.

5. UGC-NET

UGC-NET determines eligibility for Assistant Professor positions and/or Junior Research Fellowship according to applicable rules and subject requirements.

Important Note

Teacher recruitment, degree qualifications, TET requirements and institutional rules differ by teaching level and employer. Passing an eligibility test alone does not guarantee appointment.

Skills Required for Education Careers

Professional Skills

  • Subject knowledge and teaching methods.
  • Lesson planning and curriculum understanding.
  • Assessment and feedback techniques.
  • Educational technology and digital tools.
  • Research and learning evaluation.
  • Classroom and programme management.

Personal Skills

  • Communication and listening.
  • Patience and empathy.
  • Creativity and adaptability.
  • Leadership and teamwork.
  • Organisation and time management.
  • Continuous learning.

How to Build a Career in Education?

  1. Choose the age group and subject you want to work with.
  2. Select a suitable recognised degree programme.
  3. Complete required teacher education qualifications where applicable.
  4. Prepare for relevant eligibility tests or recruitment examinations.
  5. Gain practical teaching or training experience.
  6. Develop digital teaching and communication skills.
  7. Apply for suitable educational roles or higher studies.
