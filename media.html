Media & Communication

Explore careers in journalism, digital media, advertising, content creation and communication.

About Media & Communication

Media and communication involve creating, presenting and sharing information, stories, ideas and messages with audiences. This field includes journalism, television, radio, films, digital media, advertising, public relations, social media and content production. It offers opportunities for creative individuals interested in writing, storytelling, communication and technology.
