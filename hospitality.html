About Hospitality & Tourism

Hospitality and tourism focus on providing quality services to guests, travellers and customers. This industry includes hotels, restaurants, airlines, resorts, travel agencies, tourism companies, events and destination management. It offers opportunities for creative, communication-oriented and management-focused individuals.
